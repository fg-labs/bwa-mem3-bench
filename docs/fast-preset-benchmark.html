Validating the --fast preset in the 0.5.0 release

The 0.5.0 --fast preset grew three new levers since first benchmark — including --adaptive-band, the fix for the SBX long-read gap this suite surfaced. Re-run across 6 datasets × 6 architectures × 5 reps vs default bwa-mem3 and minibwa, with truth-based accuracy.

fg-labs 123ce41 (v0.5.0, PR #185) preset +skip-contained-ext +max-extend-chains 5 +adaptive-band minibwa a8cf4d3 reps 5

Validation verdict

0.5.0 --fast is ship-worthy for standard DNA: ~2× faster than default, at parity-or-faster than minibwa, with WGS placement accuracy intact. The new levers roughly doubled the speedup and — via --adaptive-band — closed the SBX long-read gap to minibwa (2.2× → 1.0× on NEON). Truth-graded WGS placement is unchanged from default despite a large drop in raw concordance, confirming the divergence is benign reshuffling. One caveat: the aggressive levers cost methylation placement ~0.9 pt vs default — worth tempering under --meth.

The SBX result — --adaptive-band closed the gap

SBX single-end, NEON: 2.2× behind minibwa → parity

This suite root-caused the SBX slowness to bwa-mem3's sse2neon-translated banded-SW kernel scaling badly on long reads. 0.5.0's --adaptive-band (#194) — start tight, expand the SW band to chain geometry on long-extension reads — targets exactly that. Result on c8g: fast wall 32s → 15s, matching minibwa's 15s (f/mini 2.21× → 1.02×); c7g 2.13× → 1.02×. On x86 SBX, --fast is now faster than minibwa (c7i 0.73×). The long-read gap is gone.

Accuracy — is the extra speed free?

Two of the new levers (--max-extend-chains, --adaptive-band) are not byte-identical, so concordance-vs-default dropped sharply (wgs 96.9→94.7, panel 89→63). The question is whether truth-graded placement held. Higher correct% is better.

WGS: yes, free. Placement is within 0.06 pt of default — the large concordance-vs-default drop is reads moving to equally-valid positions, not mismaps (sim-wgs mismapped +0.06 pt only). That's exactly what a well-behaved not-byte-identical speed preset should look like.

Meth caveat for the release

On bisulfite, 0.5.0 --fast gives up ~0.9 pt of placement vs default (93.14 vs 94.27) — worse than the prior preset's ~0.3 pt — and +1.1 pt mismapped. The aggressive --max-extend-chains/--adaptive-band hurt bisulfite placement more than plain DNA. Not a blocker, but consider tempering those levers under --meth, or documenting the trade in the 0.5.0 notes.
